Use Family Link to manage the child's supported Android device, then configure YouTube separately within that supervision. Device limits control app access and time. YouTube settings control the child's viewing experience. A supervised Android phone does not automatically restrict every video to one you approved, and the parent's own phone can use a different operating system.

## Confirm the supervision relationship

Start with the child's real Google account and the device listed in Family Link. Follow Google's setup instructions for the child's age and location. Adding supervision to an existing account can have consequences for existing YouTube content, so read Google's explanation before changing the account.

If the device is not listed as supervised, changing a YouTube content setting alone will not establish device-wide limits.

## Choose the viewing experience

In Family Link, select the child, open Controls and YouTube. Decide between YouTube Kids and a supervised regular YouTube experience where available.

YouTube Kids offers a smaller catalog and profile settings. To enable Approve content yourself, Google's instructions direct you to the YouTube Kids app. Do not expect a universal individual-video approval list inside ordinary YouTube.

The [account comparison](/guides/youtube-kids-vs-supervised-account) explains the content tradeoff.

## Set app and device limits

Open Screen time, Time limits and App limits in Family Link. Select the relevant app, choose a permitted duration and save. Use a device daily limit if the rule concerns the whole device, and schedules for recurring restricted periods.

Check any apps marked as unlimited. Google documents exceptions for system apps and differences in how limits apply across devices. A household with a tablet and phone should test both rather than assume the allowance is pooled in the way the family expects.

For a one-off YouTube Kids session, its built-in timer may be simpler. The [time-limit comparison](/guides/youtube-time-limit-for-kids) covers both approaches.

## Review ordinary alternative routes

Look at whether the child can also open YouTube through a browser or another installed app. Decide which route the family intends to use, then manage the others with the device's supported controls.

A tablet shared with adults needs particular attention to account switching. The child's restricted account does not change the rules inside an adult session.

Also check the television separately. Android device supervision does not imply that every television using a Google service has identical limits.

## What LittleLoop does here

LittleLoop currently offers an iPhone and iPad player; an Android release is not available in this launch. It should therefore not be presented as the installation step for this Android setup.

Even on supported devices, it requires parent selection and uses an official YouTube embed that may show ads. It is not affiliated with Google or YouTube.

## Keep the maintenance manageable

Once the content and time settings are saved, test a short session. Confirm the chosen profile, expiration behavior and normal reopen flow. Tell the child how to request more time or a different video rather than leaving them to guess.

Revisit the setup when the device or account changes. For ongoing questions about the boundary between account and device controls, consult [what Family Link can control](/guides/youtube-family-link-parental-controls).
