Turn off Search for the child's YouTube Kids profile in the parent settings or through the linked parent's controls. The child then loses the ability to search the YouTube Kids catalog. They can still see content available through the remaining experience, including verified channels and material you shared. Search off is narrower than the default browsing setup, but it is not the same as approving every video yourself.

## Find the right profile

Open YouTube Kids, enter the parent settings and choose the profile your child actually uses. Locate Search and switch it off. If you use Family Link, select the child, open Controls and YouTube, and look under the YouTube Kids settings.

The linked parent's YouTube Family Center is another route for supported profile settings. Make the change for each child who needs it. A parent's own YouTube search remains a separate experience.

Afterward, open the child view. Confirm that the search control is absent rather than relying only on the parent settings screen.

## What happens to history

Google says turning off Search clears the profile's watch and search history and resets its recommendations and Watch it again area. If you want to review recent activity, do that before making the change.

This reset can surprise a child who expects familiar videos to remain easy to find. Consider preparing a few approved or shared favorites first. Explain that the set of choices is changing instead of telling them the app is broken.

Turning search back on later is not a reliable way to recover cleared history.

## What can still appear

Search is one path into the catalog. Recommendations and videos from verified channels are other paths. A child may therefore tap unfamiliar videos even when the search bar is gone.

The phrase "verified by YouTube Kids" does not mean "watched by you." It describes the platform's selection, not your family's individual preferences. A video can meet the platform's rules and still be too noisy, too advanced or simply unwanted in your home.

If the rule is "only these particular videos," use [Approved Content Only](/guides/youtube-kids-approved-content-only). It also disables child search while changing the underlying available set.

## A useful middle ground

Search off can work when you broadly accept the YouTube Kids catalog but want fewer open-ended choices. It may also help a young child who cannot type but keeps opening voice search.

It is less useful when the main difficulty is repeatedly choosing suggested videos you would never have selected. For that case, look at [how recommendations work](/guides/stop-youtube-recommendations-for-kids).

You can combine search restrictions with autoplay off and a viewing limit. Each setting has a different job. The [overall control guide](/guides/control-what-child-watches-on-youtube) helps choose the combination without installing unnecessary tools.

## Check other entry points

The setting concerns YouTube Kids. It does not disable search in a browser, the normal YouTube app or another streaming service. On a shared phone, return to the child's actual watching route before you consider setup complete.

If several adults hand over different devices, agree on the same profile and app. A consistent entry point usually matters more than repeatedly checking one switch on one tablet.
